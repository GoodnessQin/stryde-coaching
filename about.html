Meet Chiamaka

FOUNDER, STRYDE COACHING

Chiamaka brings 10 years of hands-on experience supporting people through some of the most defining moments of their working lives from entering new roles, navigating workplace challenges, to growing into leadership and long-term career fulfilment.

As a Career Coach and Mentor, Chiamaka brings a deep and practical understanding of how careers truly unfold within organisations — not in theory, but in real workplaces shaped by pressure, change, and competing demands.

Working closely with people every day, she has supported individuals through some of the most defining seasons of their professional lives — from navigating the ache of unfulfilling roles to rebuilding confidence, recovering from burnout, managing transitions, pursuing growth, and embracing reinvention.

She understands how personal struggles often show up in professional spaces — and how unresolved life issues can quietly limit potential. What truly sets her apart is her ability to hold space, listen deeply, and help people make sense of their experiences — without judgment and without pressure.

Chiamaka supports clients in navigating real-world challenges: career pivots, identity shifts, self-doubt, decision fatigue, workplace anxiety, leadership readiness, and life transitions that demand both courage and clarity. Her approach blends structure with compassion, strategy with self-awareness, and accountability with grace.

About Stryde Coaching

Stryde Coaching supports professionals in accessing the clarity and confidence they already carry within but cannot fully access. I meet them exactly where they are—often tangled in the weight of their own voices, doubts, and pressures—and help them transform, creating sustainable momentum in their careers and lives.

My coaching approach is structured yet reflective, combining honest self-evaluation with practical action. Every session is approached with care, curiosity, and an expectation that real growth is possible no matter where someone is starting from.

I don't offer quick fixes. I help you build capacity; emotionally, mentally, and practically, so you can move forward with a measured, consistent, and sustainable momentum all within you are becoming.
